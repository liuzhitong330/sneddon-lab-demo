# Sneddon Lab research demo

An interactive, paper-grounded map of pancreatic endocrine differentiation, prepared by Cathy Liu for the Sneddon Lab at UCSF.

The August 2026 redesign follows Cathy's university-lab editorial system: a compact serif research narrative with target-specific developmental trajectory, lineage-tracing, and branch-marker interactions. It organizes reported results from Byrnes et al. (2018) and GSE101099; it is not a raw-data reanalysis.
